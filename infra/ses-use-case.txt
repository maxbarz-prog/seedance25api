Remerged (https://remerged.ai) generates video from a text prompt using
third-party model APIs and resells that capacity at cost. A member signs up,
buys credits, submits a prompt, and a rendering job runs for one to several
minutes. We are asking for production access in us-east-1 at the default
quota. We do not need a raised sending rate — the sandbox's restriction to
verified addresses is the only thing in our way, because it means a member
who signs up cannot receive the notification for the video they just made.

VERIFIED IDENTITY
remerged.ai is a verified domain identity in us-east-1: Easy DKIM enabled
with DkimAttributes.Status = SUCCESS and three CNAMEs published; SPF at the
apex (v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all); DMARC at _dmarc.remerged.ai
(v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:support@remerged.ai); and MX at the apex
(10 inbound-smtp.us-east-1.amazonaws.com), so the domain also receives mail
through SES. All mail is sent from no-reply@remerged.ai, and
support@remerged.ai is a real mailbox a person reads — SES receives it, stores
it in S3, and a Lambda forwards it to the operator's inbox. Replies and
complaints reach a human.

HOW OFTEN WE SEND
Rarely, and never on a schedule. Every message is triggered by an action a
member took in the product. There is no campaign, broadcast, digest or
recurring send of any kind. The service has not launched and has no members;
at launch we expect well under 100 messages a day. The only sends on this
account so far are a few dozen of our own tests.

WHAT WE SEND — the complete list, with the content as sent

1. "Your video is ready" — one message when a rendering job the member started
   has finished. This is the only message a member normally receives.

     From:    Remerged <no-reply@remerged.ai>
     Subject: Your Remerged video is ready
     Body:
       Your 5s video is ready:
       https://remerged.ai/jobs/7f3c1d2e

       "a paper boat drifting down a rain-filled gutter at night"

   The duration, link and quoted prompt are the member's own job.

2. Password reset, for accounts using our built-in email-and-password login.

     From:    Remerged <no-reply@remerged.ai>
     Subject: Reset your Remerged password
     Body:
       Use this link within the next hour to choose a new password:

       https://remerged.ai/reset?token=<single-use token, valid one hour>

       If you didn't ask for this, you can ignore it.

There is no third message type. Sign-in codes and email verification are sent
by our authentication provider (Clerk) from its own infrastructure, not
through SES.

HOW WE MAINTAIN THE RECIPIENT LIST
There is no list. Addresses live on account records in DynamoDB, one row per
member, and an address gets there exactly one way: the person typed it into
our sign-up form and confirmed it. Sign-up runs through Clerk, which verifies
the address before the account becomes usable, so we never hold an
unconfirmed address. We have never purchased, rented, imported or scraped a
list, and we do not send to addresses that did not register themselves.
Deleting an account removes the row and with it any possibility of mail;
deletion and deactivation are both self-service in the product.

BOUNCES, COMPLAINTS AND UNSUBSCRIBES
Bounces: the account-level suppression list is enabled for BOUNCE, so SES
suppresses an address that hard-bounces and we never attempt it again. We do
not retry failed sends — each message is tied to one completed job and has no
value once stale.
Complaints: suppression is also enabled for COMPLAINT, so a recipient marking
a message as spam stops all further mail to that address immediately, at the
SES level, with no action needed from us.
Monitoring: bounce and complaint rates are watched through the AWS/SES
CloudWatch metrics alongside Send and Delivery. At our volume a single bounce
is visible.
Unsubscribes: both message types are transactional — one confirms work the
member paid for and asked for, the other is a password reset they requested
seconds earlier — so neither carries a marketing unsubscribe link and there is
no list to unsubscribe from. A member who wants no mail at all can deactivate
or delete their account, which stops everything. If you would prefer a
per-member switch for the completion notification, we will add one.
