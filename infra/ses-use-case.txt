Reply to case 178878528300157 — additional information about our use case.

Since that case was opened, the service has moved to its own domain
(remerged.ai) and the verified identity you asked for is in place. Answers to
each of your questions follow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED IDENTITY

remerged.ai is a verified DOMAIN identity in us-east-1:
  - Easy DKIM enabled, DkimAttributes.Status = SUCCESS, three CNAMEs
    published (<token>._domainkey.remerged.ai -> <token>.dkim.amazonses.com)
  - SPF at the apex: v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all
  - DMARC at _dmarc.remerged.ai: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:support@remerged.ai
  - MX at the apex: 10 inbound-smtp.us-east-1.amazonaws.com, so the domain
    also receives mail through SES

All mail is sent from no-reply@remerged.ai, and support@remerged.ai is a real
mailbox that a person reads (SES receives it, stores it in S3 and a Lambda
forwards it to the operator's inbox). Replies and complaints reach a human.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE SERVICE IS

Remerged (https://remerged.ai) generates video from a text prompt using
third-party model APIs, and resells that capacity at cost. A member signs up,
buys credits, submits a prompt, and a rendering job runs for one to several
minutes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW OFTEN WE SEND

Rarely, and never on a schedule. Every message is triggered by an action a
member took in the product; there is no campaign, broadcast, digest or
recurring send of any kind.

The service has not launched and has no members. At launch we expect well
under 100 messages a day. Our sandbox quota of 200/day has not been
approached: the only sends on this account so far are a few dozen of our own
tests. We are asking for production access because the recipients will be
real members, not because we need volume.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE SEND (the complete list, with the actual content)

1. "Your video is ready" — one message when a rendering job the member
   started has finished. This is the only message a member normally receives.

     From:    Remerged <no-reply@remerged.ai>
     Subject: Your Remerged video is ready
     Body:
       Your 5s video is ready:
       https://remerged.ai/jobs/7f3c1d2e

       "a paper boat drifting down a rain-filled gutter at night"

   (The duration, link and quoted prompt are the member's own job.)

2. Password reset, for accounts using our built-in email-and-password login.

     From:    Remerged <no-reply@remerged.ai>
     Subject: Reset your Remerged password
     Body:
       Use this link within the next hour to choose a new password:

       https://remerged.ai/reset?token=<single-use token, 1 hour>

       If you didn't ask for this, you can ignore it.

There is no third message type. Sign-in codes and email verification are sent
by our authentication provider (Clerk) from its own infrastructure, not
through SES.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW WE MAINTAIN THE RECIPIENT LIST

There is no list. Addresses live on account records in DynamoDB, one row per
member, and an address gets there exactly one way: the person typed it into
our sign-up form and confirmed it. Sign-up goes through Clerk, which verifies
the address before the account becomes usable, so we never hold an
unconfirmed address.

We have never purchased, rented, imported, scraped or inherited a list, and
we do not send to addresses that did not register themselves. We do not
send to role addresses or to addresses harvested from anywhere.

Deleting an account removes the row, and with it any possibility of mail.
Account deletion is self-service in the product (Account -> Delete account),
as is deactivation, which stops all activity while keeping the library.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW WE HANDLE BOUNCES, COMPLAINTS AND UNSUBSCRIBES

Bounces: the account-level suppression list is enabled for BOUNCE, so SES
suppresses an address that hard-bounces and we do not attempt it again. We do
not retry failed sends, because each message is tied to one completed job and
has no value once stale.

Complaints: the suppression list is also enabled for COMPLAINT, so a
recipient marking a message as spam stops all further mail to that address
immediately, at the SES level, without any action on our side.

Monitoring: bounce and complaint rates are watched through the AWS/SES
CloudWatch metrics, alongside Send and Delivery. Our sending volume is small
enough that a single bounce is visible.

Unsubscribes: both message types are transactional — one confirms work the
member paid for and asked for, the other is a password reset they requested
seconds earlier — so neither carries a marketing unsubscribe link, and there
is no list to unsubscribe from. A member who wants no mail at all can
deactivate or delete the account, which stops everything. If you would prefer
we add a per-member switch for the job-completion notification, we will add
one; it is a small change and we would rather do it than send mail anyone
finds unwelcome.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARE ASKING FOR

Production access in us-east-1, at the default quota. We do not need a raised
sending rate; the sandbox restriction to verified addresses is the only thing
in our way, because it means a member who signs up cannot receive the
notification for the video they just generated.
