Remerged (https://remerged.ai) is an at-cost AI video generation service.
SES carries transactional mail only, to people who created an account on the
site.

What we send:
1. "Your video is ready" — one message when a rendering job the member
   started has finished, linking to that job. This is the only SES message a
   member normally receives.
2. Password resets, for accounts using the built-in email-and-password login.
   Sign-in, email verification and reset mail for accounts using our auth
   provider (Clerk) are sent by Clerk, not SES.

Who we send to: only addresses belonging to accounts registered on the site.
No marketing, newsletters, digests or announcements. No purchased, rented,
scraped or imported lists, and no mail to addresses that did not register
themselves.

Volume: very low. The service has not launched and has no members yet. Each
message corresponds to one video a member generated, so we expect well under
100 messages a day at launch.

Bounces and complaints: the account-level suppression list is enabled for
both BOUNCE and COMPLAINT, so SES drops sends to addresses that have
hard-bounced or complained. Bounce and complaint rates are watched in
CloudWatch. Every send is triggered by an action the member took, and
deleting the account — a self-service option in the product — stops all mail
immediately.

Authentication: remerged.ai is a verified domain identity with Easy DKIM
(three CNAMEs), SPF (v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all) and DMARC
(v=DMARC1; p=none) published in DNS.

Inbound: we also receive support@remerged.ai through SES email receiving,
which stores each message in S3 and forwards it to the operator's mailbox.
